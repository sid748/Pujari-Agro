Do you manufacture PUJARI Chaff Cutter Parts in-house across your facilities?
Yes. All parts are manufactured entirely in-house across our multiple production units spanning over 1,50,000 sq. ft. Every stage of processing is carried out within our facilities, ensuring complete control over quality and consistency.
Under the leadership of Mr. Aditya Garg, our dedicated R&D team continuously works on enhancing the life, durability and performance of each part. A specialised quality control team inspects every single component to ensure it meets strict dimensional accuracy and reliability standards.
Our dynamic sales and support team provides end-to-end assistance, ensuring that every product manufactured by Vijay Industries upholds the high standards we have maintained for over 61+ years.
